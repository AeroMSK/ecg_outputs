DIAGNOSIS 1 — CURRENT PREPROCESSING
============================================================

Current preprocessing is approximately:

    physical mV
      ->
    500 Hz -> 100 Hz
      ->
    0.5–40 Hz Butterworth
      ->
    10-second window
      ->
    per-lead z-normalization

This is useful for rhythm classification.

However, per-window z-normalization can suppress absolute amplitude information
that may be useful for ST-segment depression/elevation.

Therefore, DO NOT replace the current preprocessing.

Instead implement a dual-view system.
